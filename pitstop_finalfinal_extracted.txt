Event Description 
Event Overview: 
Pit Stop Protocol is a multi-round strategy and coordination event that tests participants' logical reasoning, 
clue-solving ability, teamwork, and composure under time pressure. Inspired by the world of Formula 1 
racing, the event challenges teams to move through a qualifying assessment, a clue-driven pursuit across 
campus which also includes simple technical clues, and a final hands-on coordination challenge modelled on a 
race pit stop. While the theme is motorsport, every round is self-contained and logic-driven — no prior 
knowledge of Formula 1, its teams, or its rules is required to compete or to win. The event is designed so that 
participants who have never watched a race compete on equal footing with those who have. 
 
Event Structure & Rules: 
Round 1: Grid Qualifiers (Online Assessment) 
● Conducted online; individual team members may attempt together on one device or as decided by 
coordinators.  
● Numerical and logical reasoning questions, lightly themed c around race strategy (pit timing, lap math, 
probability).  
● All data required to solve a question is provided within it — no outside F1 knowledge is needed.  
● Teams are ranked by score ,time and shortlisted for Round 2. 
 
Round 2: Pit Lane Pursuit (Treasure Hunt) 
● Each team receives an opening chit containing a hint to the location of the next clue.  

--- PAGE 2 ---
● Teams follow the resulting chain of clues across the venue; each chit leads to the next.  
● Some clues may include a short technical, logical, or engineering-based task that must be solved to 
proceed to the next clue. All information required to solve such tasks will be provided within the clue 
itself. 
● The final clue leads to a hidden 'suspense object' — locating and presenting it marks the hunt as complete. 
 ● Teams are shortlisted based on the total time taken to complete the full chain. 
 ● Each team receives a unique set of clues to prevent teams from following one another. 
Optional Lifeline 
• Each team may use one additional hint at any point during the round. 
• The lifeline is separate from the normal clue chain. 
• If the lifeline is used, 45 sec  is added to the actual time. 
• Final Time = Actual Time + 45 sec  (if lifeline is used). 
 
Round 3 – Blindfolded Pit Walk (Final Round) 
● One member of each team is blindfolded at the start line of a hurdle track.  
● The track includes obstacles such as tyres, barricades, and cones laid out in a fixed pattern.  
● Remaining team members stand outside the track boundary and may only guide the blindfolded member 
verbally — no physical contact or touching is allowed.  
● The blindfolded member must complete the track, avoiding all hurdles, in the shortest possible time. 
● Each hurdle contact adds a time penalty; a set number of contacts results in elimination. 
● Winner is declared based on final completion time (including penalties). 
 
Lifeline Decision 
• Use Lifeline: Teams may call for an additional hint when stuck. 45 sec is added to their final time. 
• Save Lifeline: Teams may choose to solve the challenge without using the lifeline. No time penalty is 
applied, and the saved lifeline carries into the final round as a Round 3 advantage.

Event Rules and Regulations

● Each team must consist of exactly [4] members. 
● Teams must report on time for registration and briefing. 
● Only registered team members are allowed to participate in subsequent 
rounds .
