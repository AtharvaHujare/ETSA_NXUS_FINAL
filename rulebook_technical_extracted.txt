Description:- 
PCCOE Got Talent is a fun-filled technical talent and innovation showcase that gives students a platform to showcase 
their technical skills, creativity, humour, and innovative ideas in front of a live audience and a panel of judges.  
Participants can bring their unique technical talents to the stage, including coding, AI, technical builds, digital 
design, app/website development, technical storytelling, and other technology -based creations. Judges interact 
with participants through fun questions, reactions , and a simple 1–10 rating system , making the event both 
engaging and interactive.  The focus is on technical creativity, originality, innovation, presentation, and creating 
an enjoyable experience for everyone. Each showcase will have a maximum time of 4 minutes. The event aims 
to provide students with a fun platform beyond academics to explore, create, innovate, and confidently showcase 
their technical talents in an energetic college environment.

Judging and Evaluation Parameters:- 
Each judge gives the participant a score out of 10 based on their overall performance. 
 
Rating: 1–10 
• Each participant will receive an overall score out of 10 from the judging panel. 
• Before the judges reveal their scores, the participant will be asked to predict/guess their own score. 
• The participant's predicted score will be compared with the actual average score given by the judges. 
• Judges will consider technical skill, creativity, innovation, originality, technical understanding, 
implementation/functionality, and presentation while assigning the score.  
• The average score of all judges will be considered the participant's final technical showcase score. 
• The self-prediction is an interactive element and will not affect the participant's actual judging score. 
• In case of a tie, the organizing committee may use audience response and judges' discussion as tie-breaker 
criteria.

Rules & Regulations:- 
 
PCCOE Got Talent is a technical talent and innovation showcase designed to provide students with a platform to 
showcase their technical skills, creativity, innovation, originality, technology-driven ideas and stage presence. 
 
1. Eligibility 
1. The event is open to currently enrolled PCCOE students. 
2. Participants may participate individually or as a team of up to 4 members. 
3. Each participant/team must complete the registration process within the specified deadline. 
4. A participant may participate in more than one act only if permitted by the organizing committee. 
 
2. Performance Rules 
1. Participants may showcase talents such as: 
o Unique Projects & Technical Builds 
o AI & AI Creativity 
o Tech-Based Skits & Storytelling 
o Coding & Creative Programming 
o Digital Art & Graphic Design 
o Video Editing & Digital Content Creation 
o App / Website Design 
o Innovation & Tech Ideas 
o Digital Music & Audio Production 
o Other technical talents approved by the organizers. 
2. Performances must be appropriate for a college audience. 
3. Participants must present and understand their technical work within the allotted time. 
4. The use of explicit, obscene, sexually inappropriate, discriminatory, hateful, or abusive content is 
strictly prohibited. 
Personal attacks, harassment, bullying, or defamatory remarks against students, faculty, staff, or any individual 
are not permitted. Participants are responsible for ensuring that their performance does not violate copyright or 
other applicable rights 
--- PAGE ---
 
 
 
3. Language & Content 
1. Performances may be conducted in English, Hindi, Marathi, or other languages, subject to approval by 
the organizers. 
2. Mild humour and roasting may be permitted, but it must remain within acceptable college standards. 
3. Jokes targeting an individual's religion, caste, race, gender, disability, appearance, sexuality, or other 
protected/personal characteristics are not permitted. 
4. Political, religious, or otherwise sensitive material should not be used in a manner that promotes hostility 
or targets individuals/groups. 
5. The organizing committee reserves the right to stop a performance if  its content crosses  the permitted 
boundaries. 
4. Time Limit 
1. Each participant/team will be allotted a maximum performance time of 4 minutes. 
2. Participants must complete their performance within the allotted time. 
3. Exceeding the time limit may  result in deduction of points or termination of the performance, at the 
discretion of the judges. 
4. The organizers may provide separate time limits for different rounds. 
 
5. Props & Equipment 
1. Participants must inform the organizers in advance about any special equipment or props required. 
2. Props must be safe and suitable for use inside the college premises. 
3. Fire, explosives, weapons, flammable substances, dangerous chemicals, or hazardous equipment 
are strictly prohibited. 
4. Props that may damage the stage, college property, or endanger participants/audience members will not 
be permitted. 
5. Participants may use laptops, mobile devices, electronic components, microcontrollers, sensors, robotics 
systems, prototypes, models, and other technical equipment, subject to prior approval and safety 
requirements. 
6. Participants are responsible for bringing their own specialized equipment unless otherwise specified. 
 
6. Technical Presentation & Media Requirements 
1. Participants using videos, audio, presentations, software demonstrations, or other digital media must 
submit the required files/formats as specified by the organizing committee before the event. 
2. Audio tracks containing prohibited or excessively explicit content will not be accepted. 
3. Participants should carry a backup copy of their audio/video files. 
4. Participants are responsible for ensuring that their presentations, software, demonstrations, and media 
files are functional and ready before their scheduled performance. 
 
7. Judging 
1. The judging panel will evaluate performances based on criteria such as: 
o Technical Skill & Understanding 
o Creativity 
o Innovation & Originality 
o Implementation / Functionality 
o Stage Presence 
o Presentation & Communication 
o Audience Engagement 
2. The judges' decision will be final and binding. 
3. Participants may be eliminated at any stage according to the format announced by the organizers. 
4. Judges may interact with participants and ask technical questions regarding their performance.
--- PAGE ---
 
 
8. Audience Participation 
1. Audience participation may be incorporated into selected rounds as an interactive element, but it will not 
directly determine the technical evaluation score unless announced by the organizers. 
2. Audience members must maintain appropriate behaviour and must not heckle, threaten, physically 
approach, or harass participants. 
3. Any coordinated disruption of the event may result in removal from the venue. 
9. Technical Skits, Storytelling & Humour Rules 
1. Technical skits, storytelling, and humour may be included as part of the performance, provided they are 
related to technology, innovation, or student life. 
2. Humour and roasting must remain light -hearted and must not become personal harassment or targeted 
abuse. 
3. Participants must not reveal or circulate another person's private or confidential information. 
4. Participants must not make offensive, discriminatory, or inappropriate remarks about any individual or 
group. 
5. Technical information presented during a skit or storytelling performance should be accurate and not 
intentionally misleading. 
6. The judges and organizers reserve the right to stop any content that violates the event guidelines. 
 
10. Respect & Conduct 
1. All participants are expected to maintain respectful behaviour toward judges, organizers, volunteers, 
faculty, staff, and fellow participants. 
2. Physical altercations, threats, intimidation, or harassment will  result in  immediate disqualification and 
may be referred to college authorities. 
3. Misconduct before, during, or after the event may result in disciplinary action as per college rules. 
4. Participants must follow instructions given by event coordinators and venue authorities. 
 
 
11. Disqualification 
A participant/team may be disqualified for: 
• Using prohibited or dangerous materials. 
• Providing false information regarding the originality, functionality, technical specifications, or 
contribution to a project. 
• Using unauthorized or unsafe technical equipment during a demonstration. 
• Performing content that violates the event's guidelines. 
• Misbehaving with judges, organizers, participants, or audience members. 
• Deliberately disrupting the event. 
• Providing false information during registration. 
• Exceeding performance limits repeatedly. 
• Damaging college property. 
• Engaging in plagiarism or presenting another person's work as their own. 
• Violating applicable college policies. 
Disqualification decisions made by the organizing committee will be final.
